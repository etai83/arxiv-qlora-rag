Leakage-Safe Temporal RAG on arXiv cs.CL

Date-Split Corpus Construction, bge-small-en-v1.5 Vector Ingestion, 60-Prompt 4-Slice Golden Benchmark, Calibrated Cross-Family Judging, and Zero-Shot vs. RAG Baselines.

Abstract

T = 2025-01-01 · 500 Abstracts

Evaluating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and future QLoRA hybrids requires strict separation between parametric pretraining knowledge and post-cutoff evidence. We select cutoff date T = 2025-01-01—strictly after Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct's published December 2023 training cutoff—and curate 300 pre-T (static) and 200 post-T (live) arXiv cs.CL abstracts with zero ID overlap. Indexing only the 200 post-T abstracts in FAISS (BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5) and benchmarking 60 hand-verified prompts across four slices reveals a sharp hybrid trade-off: RAG (k=4) lifts live correctness from 1.00 to 3.85 and temporal_override obedience from 0% to 70% while maintaining 5.00/5.00 abstention on adversarial unanswerable prompts, yet drops static correctness to 1.80 (20/20 forced abstentions). Calibrating a cross-family Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct judge via Chain-of-Thought field ordering and explicit abstention rules raised Judge-vs-Human agreement from 48.33% to 80.0% exact match (86.67% within-±1).

1. Four Golden Slice Types (N = 60) & Concrete Examples

static · n = 20 (g01–g20) Published < T

What it tests: Pre-cutoff cs.CL facts (15 landmark pre-Dec-2023 papers + 5 early-2024 papers) absent from the post-T RAG index. Isolates parametric retention vs. live-only RAG over-abstention.

✓ Positive Example (g11 RoPE · Zero-Shot)C=4 F=4 Cit=5

Explains RoPE rotation-matrix encoding and decaying inter-token dependency with relative distance accurately from pretraining weights.

✗ Negative Example (g01 LoRA RAG / g02 ZS)C=1 F=5 Cit=5

Live-only RAG outputs "Not in the sources." because pre-T 2106.09685 is absent from live.jsonl; Zero-Shot g02 misstates Lewis et al.'s RAG components as "Textual Memory".

live · n = 20 (g21–g40) Published >= T

What it tests: Post-cutoff (2025–2026) methods, datasets, and empirical findings guaranteed unseen during llama3.2:3b pretraining. Isolates dense retrieval + [arxiv_id] citation grounding.

✓ Positive Example (g37 MindEdit-Bench · RAG)C=5 F=5 Cit=5

Accurately reports the 6-task world-model hierarchy and 8%–31% vs. 81%–97% (53 pp gap) on L4/L5 counterfactuals, citing [2607.00491].

✗ Negative Example (g28 LitBench · Zero-Shot)C=1 F=5 Cit=5

Falsely abstains ("I don't have information on LitBench...") due to Dec 2023 cutoff instead of retrieving post-T evidence (or scores 1/1/1 if hallucinating).

temporal_override · n = 10 (g41–g50) Post-T Beats Pre-T

What it tests: Current-state queries ("What is the current best approach to X?") where post-T evidence beats or refutes earlier paradigms. Isolates evidence-over-prior conflict resolution.

✓ Positive Example (g41 SOLAR · RAG)C=5 F=5 Cit=5 Ov=1

Reports LRU/LFU underperforming FIFO on semantic buffers and adopts SOLAR (≤3 competitive ratio, 5–75% gain), citing [2607.00394].

✗ Negative Example (g44 "Aha!" Shifts · RAG / g41 ZS)C=1..3 Ov=0

Zero-shot refuses/falls back to older priors (Ov=0), and RAG g44 falsely abstains despite retrieving [2601.00514] at rank 1 (C=1, Ov=0).

unanswerable · n = 10 (g51–g60) Not in Sources

What it tests: 6 out-of-corpus prompts + 4 near-miss distractors matching indexed papers (BaseRT, MSQA, etc.) while asking unstated metrics. Isolates abstention precision.

✓ Positive Example (g59 MSQA Near-Miss · RAG)C=5 F=5 Cit=5

Retrieves 2607.00724 at rank 1 (0.799 cosine), detects Swahili accuracy & annotator counts are absent, and replies cleanly: "Not in the sources."

✗ Negative Example (Distractor Hallucination Archetype)C=1 F=1 Cit=2

Observed 0/20 times in baseline runs; would occur if the model fabricates Swahili accuracy/annotator counts and cites top-4 distractor [2607.00724].

2. Evaluation Metrics, Anchors & Agreement Gate

Metric Scale / Scope Score 5 (or 1 / Pass) Score 1 (or 0 / Fail) Design Rationale
correctness 1–5 int · All slices Accurate core claim vs. reference; or clean abstention on unanswerable. Wrong/contradictory, hallucinated answer on unanswerable, or false abstention on answerable slice. Measures end-task utility while enforcing slice-appropriate abstention.
faithfulness 1–5 int · All slices Every claim supported by retrieved_context, or pure abstention (including zero-shot NONE). Asserts ungrounded factual claims not in retrieved_context (incl. zero-shot factual answers). Decouples context grounding from parametric correctness.
citation_accuracy 1–5 int · All slices Every key claim cites valid supporting [arxiv_id] from context, or clean abstention with []. Uncited factual claims, hallucinated IDs outside valid_context_ids, or abstaining with unrelated citations. Audits verifiability and catches fabricated or mismatched IDs.
override_obedience {0, 1} · Override only 1 iff response adopts/reports newer post-T finding (newer_paper_id) as current best. 0 when reverting to older pre-T baseline, hedging inconclusively, or abstaining. Isolates temporal authority over stale model priors.
latency_ms ms · Mean & Median Sequential warm-model wall time (retrieval_ms + generation_ms in RAG). Measured without concurrency contention; cold model-load excluded via warmup. Quantifies real-time retrieval vs. generation cost trade-off.
judge_agreement % · N=20 (60 pairs) exact_match_pct ≥ 70% (J == H), plus within_1_pct (|J-H| ≤ 1) & 3-bucket match. Below 70% triggers mandatory rubric/judge calibration before trusting results. Prevents uncalibrated LLM-as-a-Judge bias from skewing findings.

3. Agents Used: Roles & How They Achieve Them

Dense Retriever Agent bge-small + FAISS

Role: Non-parametric evidence lookup over post-T (>= 2025-01-01) literature.

How it achieves it: Prepends asymmetric BGE query instruction, L2-normalizes 384-d vectors, runs exact IndexFlatIP scan (~41 ms), and applies published_date >= after prior to slicing [:k].

Generator / Baseline Agent llama3.2:3b

Role: Controlled answer synthesis in Zero-Shot vs. Strict Grounded RAG (k=4).

How it achieves it: Executes sequentially on Mac mini Metal GPU (temperature=0.0, num_predict=384); RAG prompt enforces [arxiv_id] bracket citations or exact "Not in the sources." abstention.

Cross-Family Judge Agent qwen2.5:7b

Role: Automated rubric grading across all 120 runs without same-family bias.

How it achieves it: Emits format="json" (num_ctx=4096) with CoT rationale first, 2-step pure-vs-hedged abstention rules, regex fence stripping, and clamping/slice enforcement.

Blinded Human-Benchmark Auditor 20 Stratified Items

Role: Independent ground-truth audit validating the ≥70% rubric trust gate.

How it achieves it: Grades blind_sample_20.jsonl (10 ZS + 10 RAG, zero judge leakage), exposing v1 abstention mis-scoring (48.33%) and verifying v2 calibration (80.0% exact / 86.67% ±1).

Sample Mode / Slice Human (C/F/Cit/Ov) Judge (C/F/Cit/Ov) Human Note & Judge Rationale

1–5 Ordinal Anchors

Correctness (slice-aware): 5 = accurate claim (or clean abstention on unanswerable); 1 = wrong, fabricated, or false abstention on static/live/temporal_override.

Faithfulness: 5 = strictly supported by retrieved_context OR clean abstention (even when context is NONE); 1 = ungrounded claims (including zero-shot factual answers).

Citation Accuracy: 5 = every key claim cites valid supporting [arxiv_id] OR clean abstention with empty citations; 1 = uncited claims or hallucinated IDs.

Override Obedience (0/1): 1 iff temporal_override answer adopts the newer post-T finding (newer_paper_id).

Calibration Fixes Applied in v2

  • CoT Field Order: Moved rationale first in JSON schema so qwen2.5:7b evaluates abstention & slice rules before outputting integers.
  • Pure vs. Hedged Abstention: Separated pure refusals (e.g. "I don't have information...") from hedged overviews ("I don't have exact numbers, however X works by...").
  • Few-Shot Abstention Anchors: Added explicit JSON examples mapping answerable-slice abstentions to correctness=1, faithfulness=5, citation_accuracy=5.

What QLoRA Will (and Will Not) Change

Will Change (Weights + Hybrid Routing): Fine-tuning Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct on pre-T static.jsonl internalizes foundational cs.CL mechanisms and metrics, trains calibrated confidence fallback when live FAISS similarity is low, sharpens [arxiv_id] citation discipline, and removes spurious trailing abstentions.

Will NOT Change (Live Retrieval Dependency): Post-T knowledge (>= 2025-01-01) cannot be memorized in static weights without continual retraining; fresh papers and temporal_override updates will always require retrieve(query, k, after=T).

Appendix B · Future Research Agenda

Three Research Continuations Beyond Baselines

Concrete experimental extensions targeting the three empirical bottlenecks measured in baseline evaluation: live-only static collapse (1.80), residual temporal override misses (3/10), and abstract-only truncation (3.85 live).

Continuation 1 Static 1.80 → ≥4.20

Confidence-Gated Hybrid Routing + Pre-T QLoRA

Baseline Bottleneck: Live-only RAG abstained 20/20 on static (1.80), while Zero-Shot refused 18/20 (1.45).

Proposed Method: Train 4-bit NF4 QLoRA adapters on corpus/static.jsonl QA pairs, then gate inference via a calibrated bge-reranker-v2-m3 score τ: high τ enforces grounded post-T RAG; mid-band near-miss distractors (u07–u10) abstain; low τ routes to [parametric < T] QLoRA weights.

Hypothesis: Eliminates live-only blindspot without breaking unanswerable precision.
Continuation 2 Override 70% → ≥95%

Temporal Reranking & Counterfactual Conflict DPO

Baseline Bottleneck: 3/10 temporal_override misses (g44, g46, g48) even at 10/10 rank-1 FAISS recall; static + live mixed indices lack date-aware ranking.

Proposed Method: Pair hybrid BM25+dense RRF and date-decay reranking (exp(λ · Δt) on current-state queries) with Direct Preference Optimization on synthetic conflict pairs (pre-T chunk + post-T chunk), preferring explicit post-T updates over hedging or abstention.

Hypothesis: Resolves contradictory multi-era evidence deterministically.
Continuation 3 Live 3.85 → ≥4.50

Section-Aware Full-Paper Ingestion & Submodular Packing

Baseline Bottleneck: Abstract-only chunks (175-word median) omit hyperparameter & ablation tables probed by near-miss prompts (u07–u10) and cause partial omissions (g27).

Proposed Method: Parse ar5iv HTML/LaTeX full papers into hierarchical section + table chunks, retrieve via bge-m3 multi-vector scoring, and pack context via budgeted submodular maximization (cf. 2607.00725) at fixed 1,500-token reader budgets.

Hypothesis: Unlocks table/ablation QA while curbing token-budget truncation.
